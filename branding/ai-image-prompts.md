## Tips for Best Results

1. **Midjourney**: Append `--ar 1:1` for icons, `--ar 16:9` for hero illustrations
2. **DALL-E**: Specify "flat design, vector style, no 3D, no photorealism"
3. **Ideogram**: Good for text-in-image (logo with "Brite Shopping" text)
4. Generate multiple variants and pick the cleanest one
5. After generation, upscale to required dimensions with an AI upscaler if needed
6. For the app icon, test at small sizes (render at 60x60) to ensure readability
